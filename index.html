No referral is needed to see a GP at any Qualitas clinic. For specialist consultations, a GP referral is recommended for insurance purposes. Your GP can usually issue a same-day referral if needed.
We accept all major Singapore insurance plans including AIA, Prudential, Great Eastern, NTUC Income, AXA, Manulife, Aviva, Cigna, HSBC Life, and MediShield Life. Direct billing is available at all clinics — present your insurance card at registration and we handle the claim.
Yes. All Qualitas clinics share a unified patient record system. Whether you visit Orchard, Tampines, Novena or any other location, your doctor will have access to your full consultation history, test results, and prescriptions.
Most Qualitas clinics are open Monday to Friday 8:30am–9pm and Saturday 8:30am–1pm. Select locations including our Raffles Place clinic are open 24 hours, and Tampines is open on Sundays. Use the clinic finder to check hours at your nearest location.
You can book online via our website or app, call your preferred clinic directly, or simply walk in. Teleconsultations are available via video call through the Qualitas patient portal — ideal for follow-ups and prescription renewals.
Yes. We offer packages starting from $88, including basic blood panels, executive screenings, cancer marker tests, and comprehensive packages tailored by age and gender. Results are reviewed in-clinic with your doctor the same day.
