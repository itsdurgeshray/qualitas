About B2Core

What is B2Core?

Understanding the platform before comparing it.

B2Core is the flagship CRM and back-office platform from B2Broker. First launched in 2014, it has grown into an enterprise-grade product covering CRM, client cabinet, payment processing, IB management, and multi-currency wallets. It supports MT4, MT5, and crypto trading environments, and connects with a wide range of liquidity and payment providers.

B2Core's primary draw is ecosystem consolidation. Brokerages already operating within the B2Broker infrastructure stack often choose B2Core because it reduces integration complexity across payments, liquidity, and back-office management.

Who B2Core Is Built For

Enterprise-scale brokerages, multi-asset and crypto-focused firms, and operations already embedded in the B2Broker ecosystem. It requires dedicated technical capacity to get the most out of its customisation depth.

B2Core Key Capabilities
  • Client cabinet with customisable trader interface
  • Multi-currency wallets supporting fiat and crypto
  • KYC and AML compliance workflows
  • Multi-level IB module with large referral structures
  • MT4, MT5, and crypto exchange integrations
  • Mobile app (iOS and Android)
  • Financial reporting and reconciliation tools
  • Transaction monitoring and security controls

Why Brokers Switch from B2Core to AltimaCRM

The switch usually happens when the brokerage's main challenge shifts from infrastructure setup to conversion speed, retention, and operational visibility.

📞

The Lead Conversion Gap

Sales teams handling high outbound call volumes lose momentum when the dialer sits outside the CRM. Agents constantly move between platforms to call, log activity, and manage follow-ups. Response times slow down, visibility drops, and lead leakage increases over time.

AltimaCRM keeps VoIP, communication history, follow-ups, and client activity inside one connected workflow, so sales teams move faster without juggling multiple systems.

🛡️

Sales and Compliance in One System

In regulated brokerages, onboarding, KYC, payment activity, and communication history are tightly connected. When those workflows live across separate systems, audit preparation becomes slower and operational coordination becomes increasingly manual.

AltimaCRM keeps KYC, AML workflows, payment records, and communication activity inside the same platform, so compliance teams work from a connected audit trail instead of piecing data together manually.

📊

Real-Time Brokerage Visibility

As brokerages scale, operational reporting often becomes fragmented. Sales performance, onboarding status, IB activity, and compliance reporting all sit in different dashboards managed by different teams.

AltimaCRM gives leadership a centralised view across sales, retention, compliance, finance, and IB management — making it easier to track brokerage performance in real time without disconnected reports.
