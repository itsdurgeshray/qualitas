Is my insurance plan accepted at Qualitas?
We accept over 100 insurance plans including AXA, Prudential, AIA, Great Eastern, NTUC Income, Cigna, and Allianz. Direct billing means you typically pay nothing out-of-pocket for covered treatments. Use the insurance checker on this page or call our front desk to confirm before your visit.
How do I book as a new patient?
Click "Book Appointment," create a free patient account in under 2 minutes, then choose your preferred clinic, doctor, and time slot. You can also call any clinic directly or walk in during opening hours. No referral letter is needed for GP visits.
Are your doctors experienced with expat patients?
Absolutely. Many of our patients are expatriates from the UK, USA, Australia, India, and across Europe and Asia. All our doctors are fluent in English and several speak additional languages. We also handle international insurance and provide medical reports for visa and employment pass requirements.
Can I get a referral to a specialist?
Yes. Our GPs can provide specialist referrals within our network or to major Singapore hospitals. We also coordinate pre-authorization with your insurance provider. For non-urgent cases we aim to have you seen by a specialist within one week.
Do you accept CHAS and Medisave?
Yes. Our GP clinics are accredited for CHAS, providing subsidies for Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents with Blue, Orange, or Green CHAS cards. Medisave can be used under the Chronic Disease Management Programme. Coverage varies by condition and card type, so please confirm at your clinic.
What should I bring to my first appointment?
Please bring your NRIC, passport, or Employment Pass; your insurance card or provider name; any current medications; and relevant previous medical records if available. Arriving 10 minutes early helps ensure a smooth check-in.
