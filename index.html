Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about AltimaCRM RMS from risk managers and compliance teams.

A Risk Management System (RMS) is the operational layer that sits between your trading data and your risk decisions. It monitors trading behavior, account exposure, client identity patterns, and operational activity across your brokerage, and helps your team identify, investigate, and act on emerging risks before they become costly problems. For forex brokers and prop firms, RMS is the difference between reacting to risk after the fact and managing it in real time.
A standard trading monitor shows you what is happening. RMS helps you understand why, and what to do about it. Most monitoring tools generate alerts and stop there. AltimaCRM RMS goes further: it connects behavioral signals, identity data, exposure patterns, and operational activity into a complete risk lifecycle — from detection through investigation, enforcement, and audit. It is not a dashboard. It is an intelligence and governance framework.
