Collaboration

Communication

Ownership

Culture values

What we hold each other to.

01

Craft over speed

We’d rather ship it right once than fast three times.

02

Say the hard thing

Kind, direct feedback beats polite silence every time.

03

Own the outcome

Not the task, the result. Follow it through to launch and after.

04

Learn in public

Share what you figured out. Write it down for the next person.

05

Leave room for life

Sustainable pace is a feature, not a perk.
